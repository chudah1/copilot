# Before Hindsight content strategy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

## 1. Objective

Build an audience of self-directed investors who care about how they make and
review decisions. Earn attention and trust through useful investing content
before introducing penKeep.

The account is not a stock-picking page. It does not tell people what to buy,
hold, or sell.

## 2. Editorial identity

**Account:** Before Hindsight

**Bio:**

> Investment psychology, research and decision-making.  
> No stock tips.

**Editorial promise:**

> The news is the hook. The investment thesis is the story.

Before Hindsight examines the decisions behind investment outcomes: what an
investor believed, which evidence mattered, what changed, and what remained
unresolved.

## 3. Target audience

The primary reader:

- owns or researches individual companies;
- reads earnings, filings, company news, or investment research;
- wants to make their own decisions rather than receive tips;
- has felt fear, regret, overconfidence, or uncertainty around a position;
- wants to improve their process over years.

Content should not be optimised for an audience primarily seeking daily trade
signals, crypto speculation, luxury motivation, or generic budgeting advice.

## 4. Content mix

### 50% — Current-event thesis tests

Use a recent earnings release, filing, management change, regulatory event,
product development, competitive move, or material news story.

The post should answer:

- What happened?
- What did investors appear to expect?
- Which part of a plausible investment case did the evidence touch?
- What changed?
- What did not change?
- What should an investor monitor next?

These posts create discovery because people are already searching for the
company or event.

### 40% — Evergreen decision stories

Tell emotionally recognisable stories about:

- hindsight bias;
- thesis drift;
- panic after a price decline;
- conviction becoming identity;
- avoiding a difficult review;
- interpreting earnings;
- knowing when evidence matters;
- confusing a good company with a good investment.

These posts build long-term trust and give the account a distinct point of view.

### 10% — Account philosophy and community

Explain what Before Hindsight stands for, answer comments, publish audience
questions, and share reusable decision templates.

The account manifesto should be published after two value-first posts and then
pinned:

> Most investing content tells you what to buy.  
> This account is about how you decide.

### Format assignment

Two production formats now exist: carousels (this account's original
format) and video (`content/tiktok/videos.mjs`).

- **Current-event thesis tests default to video.** These posts benefit most
  from reach — people are already searching for the company or event — and
  video reaches roughly 5–6x further than a carousel. Publish within the
  existing 24-hour window (Section 6, Step 7) while search interest is live.
- **Evergreen decision stories default to carousels.** These reward saves
  and re-reading; carousels get roughly 2–3x more saves and comments than
  video for the same idea.
- **Crossover is allowed either direction** when an idea's shape calls for
  it — this is a rule of thumb, not a hard rule.

## 5. The story model

Every carousel must work as one coherent story, not eight isolated facts.

### Eight-slide default

1. **Emotional hook** — a moment the reader recognises.
2. **Situation** — establish what the investor believed or felt.
3. **Tension** — introduce the event, doubt, or conflicting evidence.
4. **Reframe** — reveal the mistake or more useful question.
5. **Evidence** — show the relevant facts or comparison.
6. **Decision framework** — give practical questions.
7. **Resolution** — state the lesson in memorable language.
8. **Conversation** — invite a save, follow, or specific response.

Five-to-seven slides are allowed when the idea is simpler. No post should be
extended merely to reach eight slides.

### Emotional standard

Emotion should come from an honest investing experience — opening a
portfolio after a sharp fall, waiting for earnings, defending a long-held
position, revisiting an old trade — but it no longer needs to be
understated. Bolder hooks, sharper claims, and real urgency are allowed.

The guardrail is truthfulness, not tone: every factual claim still has to
survive Section 6 Step 6 verification before it is allowed to publish.
Loosening tone does not loosen that.

Avoid:

- claims that cannot be verified against a primary source;
- promises of wealth or superior returns;
- shame;
- engagement bait unrelated to the story.

## 6. Current-event workflow

### Step 1 — Scan

Monitor:

- company investor-relations pages;
- exchange announcements and regulatory filings;
- earnings calendars;
- Reuters, Financial Times, Bloomberg, CNBC, and reputable business reporting;
- official regulator and government announcements.

TikTok investment accounts are used to notice topics and study presentation,
not as the sole source for factual claims.

### Step 2 — Select

A story should meet at least four of these five conditions:

1. People are currently searching or talking about it.
2. It contains a real investment-thesis question.
3. It has emotional tension without requiring sensationalism.
4. The relevant facts can be verified through primary sources.
5. It can be discussed without giving a buy, hold, or sell recommendation.

### Step 3 — Source

Use this order:

1. Company filing, earnings release, transcript, or official announcement.
2. Regulator, government, or exchange source.
3. High-quality reporting for market context and competing interpretations.

Record publication dates, event dates, and links. Distinguish company claims
from independently established facts.

### Step 4 — Find the thesis question

Do not summarise the news. Translate it into a decision question.

Examples:

- Did pricing power offset weak demand, or merely delay the pressure?
- Is rising capital expenditure evidence of demand or a threat to returns?
- Did guidance change because the business improved or because expectations
  were reset?
- Does a management departure touch the original case or only create noise?

### Step 5 — Draft

Build the story around:

> expectation → evidence → tension → decision question

The market reaction may be included as context, but a price move is never
treated as proof that the investment case strengthened or broke.

### Step 6 — Verify

Before rendering:

- check every number and date;
- make sure comparisons use equivalent periods;
- distinguish reported and adjusted measures;
- confirm that the headline matches the evidence;
- remove any implied recommendation;
- include source notes and an educational disclaimer.

### Step 7 — Publish promptly

Aim to publish within 24 hours of a significant event while it remains relevant.
Accuracy takes priority over being first.

## 7. Visual system

The account uses:

- black for the opening emotional hook;
- warm cream for the narrative and explanation;
- forest green for the key insight, comparison, or closing;
- editorial serif headlines;
- simple progress numbers such as `01 / 08`;
- generous space and one main idea per slide.

Colour rhythm is purposeful, not alternating decoration:

> black hook → cream story → green emphasis → green closing

The lower portion of every image remains clear for TikTok interface overlays.

The hook — the cover slide, or a video's opening scene — may use a bolder
claim, a number, or a provocation than the rest of the post. The slides or
scenes that follow must substantiate it; a bold hook is not licence to
publish something the evidence does not support.

## 8. Captions and search

The cover carries the emotional hook. The TikTok title carries the clear,
searchable version of the idea.

Example:

- **Cover:** “You bought a brilliant company. Then the investment went wrong.”
- **Title:** “Why a Great Company Can Still Be a Bad Investment”

Captions should:

1. restate the central tension plainly;
2. add one piece of context rather than repeat every slide;
3. ask one specific question;
4. include “Educational only—not investment advice” where appropriate;
5. use three-to-five relevant hashtags.

Keep 2–3 specific, searchable hashtags. Alongside them, 1–2 broad reach tags
(`#fyp`, or a currently trending tag) are now allowed — reach is an explicit
goal. Do not replace the specific tags with broad ones; stuffing a post with
only broad tags loses the searchability this section is otherwise for.

For company stories, include the company name, ticker where useful, event, and
investment concept naturally in the title or caption.

## 9. Publishing cadence

### First 14 days

- Publish one high-quality carousel each day.
- Publish a second post only when a genuinely timely event justifies it.
- Alternate current and evergreen stories where possible.
- Do not publish several posts within minutes of one another.

Suggested opening sequence:

1. Evergreen: good company versus good investment.
2. Current event: a live thesis test.
3. Account manifesto.
4. Evergreen: price decline versus broken case.
5. Current event: earnings or guidance.
6. Evergreen: hindsight rewrites memory.
7. Current event: competitive or regulatory evidence.

### After 14 days

Use performance to decide whether to remain at one post per day or increase to
two. Volume creates more experiments; it does not compensate for weak stories.

## 10. Engagement routine

For 20–30 minutes each day:

- read and respond to substantive comments;
- ask follow-up questions rather than giving advice;
- leave thoughtful comments on relevant investing and business-news posts;
- save strong examples into Hooks, Stories, Carousels, and Questions;
- mark stock-tip, get-rich-quick, and irrelevant content as not interested;
- record recurring audience questions as future post ideas.

Do not use follow-for-follow, mass following, generic comments, or unsolicited
penKeep promotion.

## 11. Measurement

Review results after every five posts and at the end of each week.

Prioritise:

1. saves per 1,000 views;
2. shares per 1,000 views;
3. thoughtful comments;
4. profile visits per 1,000 views;
5. follows from profile visits;
6. carousel completion or reading time where available.

Follower count and raw views are secondary. A post with fewer views but strong
saves and relevant comments may be more valuable than a broad low-intent post.

For each post, record:

- current or evergreen;
- company/topic;
- hook style;
- number of slides;
- question used;
- publication time;
- metrics after 24 hours and seven days;
- what should be repeated or changed.

## 12. Paid promotion

Do not promote the opening post while the profile is nearly empty.

Consider a small Promote test only when:

- the profile contains at least five strong posts;
- a post has already shown above-average organic saves, profile visits, or
  follows;
- the post complies with TikTok advertising rules;
- its sound is licensed for commercial use.

If tested:

- start with £10–£20;
- optimise for profile visits or followers rather than raw views;
- target an adult UK audience;
- stop if paid views do not translate into profile visits or relevant follows.

## 13. Introducing penKeep

penKeep does not appear in the opening audience-building phase.

Begin the transition only when:

- the profile has a credible library of useful posts;
- several posts have earned saves or substantive comments;
- recurring audience problems clearly connect to decision records;
- the account has a recognisable editorial identity.

Transition sequence:

1. Publish a decision template with no product promotion.
2. Show how a private decision record solves a recurring audience problem.
3. State transparently that the creator is building penKeep.
4. Introduce penKeep in approximately one of every eight posts.
5. Keep the remaining posts valuable without requiring the product.

Any post directly promoting penKeep must make the commercial relationship clear
and use TikTok's applicable content-disclosure setting.

## 14. Editorial test

Before approving any idea, ask:

> Does this help an investor examine a decision, or does it merely give them
> another opinion?

If it only adds an opinion, do not publish it.
